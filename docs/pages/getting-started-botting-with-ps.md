# Botting with PS

> Source: <https://docs.project-sylvanas.net/getting-started/botting-with-ps>

> [!DANGER]
> 1. **Botting is anything your character does without you.** As a rule of thumb, more than **5 minutes** of gameplay with no real input from you is botting.
> 2. **PS Bot (PSB) is the only product to bot with.** It is a paid add-on on top of PS Base, currently 10 EUR, and buying it on the official shop is what grants you Bot Bypass.
> 3. **PS Private (PSP) is never a botting setup.** It is a different paid add-on, currently 80 EUR, it does **not** include Bot Bypass, and it was never designed for unattended play. If you want to bot, you cannot use PSP.
> 4. **Check the light before every botting session.** Install the free [Bot Bypass Checker](https://project-sylvanas.net/panel/plugins/detail/301) and read the banner in game. 🟢 GREEN means you may bot. 🔴 RED means do not bot, do not AFK, do not run automation.
> 5. **Green is not immunity.** It covers you against many of the checks the game runs, so you can bot longer with a lower chance of being caught. It is not a ban shield. Do not abuse it.

Project Sylvanas (PS) is designed to **assist you while you play**, not to take over gameplay.

Because of that, **botting with PS is not recommended by default**. If you choose to bot anyway, the rest of this page is what you need to know first.

>

## What counts as botting

Botting means letting your character play the game without you, instead of being actively controlled by a human:

- Automated farming
- AFK automation
- Repetitive scripted behavior with no real input
- Multiboxing, including a follower that does nothing but follow your main. See [Multi-Session Setup](/getting-started/multi-session-setup)

If your character is acting on its own, you are botting. It does not matter what the plugin is called or how long you intended it to run.

> [!NOTE]
> As a rule of thumb, any gameplay longer than **5 minutes** without your inputs is considered **botting**.

---

## What Bot Bypass is

Bot Bypass (also called Botting Bypass) exists **only** for botting.

When a character performs automated actions, Blizzard uses heuristic detection systems to identify non-human behavior: no real key presses, repetitive patterns, input that no person would produce. That leads to account flags and eventually to bans. Bot Bypass reduces that specific risk while you are botting. That is all it does.

It is:

- **Granted by PSB.** Buying PS Bot on the official shop is what gives you Bot Bypass. There is nothing else to request and nothing to configure to unlock it.
- **Not part of PS Base, and not part of PSP.** Neither of those covers you while botting.
- **Optional.** You do not need it to play with PS.
- **Not a general safety upgrade.** It does not make PS "safer" in any other way.
- **Only relevant if you bot.** Having "+1 bypass" does nothing while you play normally.

## You do NOT need Bot Bypass if

- You play manually
- You are actively at the keyboard
- You are not running automated behavior

Buying PSB when you do not bot is pointless. It unlocks no plugins, adds no features, and changes nothing in the Marketplace.

## What Bot Bypass is not

Bot Bypass is **not a ban shield**. It does not make you unbannable.

Being covered means many of the automated bot checks the game runs no longer have an easy answer, so you can bot for longer with a lower chance of getting banned. It does not remove the need to be sensible about session length, repetition, and frequency. Bot-like patterns still get accounts banned, covered or not.

## Check the light first: Bot Bypass Checker

**[Bot Bypass Checker on the Marketplace](https://project-sylvanas.net/panel/plugins/detail/301)**

The checker itself is **free**. Add it to your library like any other Marketplace plugin, free plugins are simply listed at 0 Gold, then refresh in game.

It does **not** perform the bypass and it does not grant anything. It only checks whether your setup is safe for botting, and shows one large banner in game:

| Banner | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| 🟢 **GREEN** | Safe to bot according to the plugin |
| 🔴 **RED** | Unsafe to bot, **or** you do not have Bot Bypass access |

Think of it as a traffic light. Install it, load in, look at the banner, and only then decide whether to start a session.

### If the banner is RED

- **DO NOT BOT**
- **DO NOT AFK**
- **DO NOT RUN AUTOMATION**

Botting while RED means you are at high risk of being flagged or banned.

Common reasons for RED:

1. You are not on **PSB** . PS Base on its own does not cover botting, and neither does PSP
2. Your botting configuration is incorrect
3. Required safety conditions are not met

> [!WARNING]
> If you are not on PSB, the banner will **always** show RED and there is nothing to fix on your side. Buy PSB on the [Subscription Panel](https://project-sylvanas.net/panel/shop/subscription), which is what grants Bot Bypass, then check the banner again.

> [!TIP]
> Check the banner at the start of every botting session, not once when you install the plugin. Your subscription, your product, and your configuration can all change between sessions, and the banner is the only thing that tells you where you actually stand.

## Responsible session guidelines

If you choose to bot, be realistic about risk. These guidelines exist to avoid the obvious suicide-botting patterns:

- **Longer than 4 to 6 hours** in one session is never recommended
- **More than 12 hours** inside a 24 hour cycle is considered **suicide botting**
- Doing very long sessions **more than once a week** dramatically increases risk

Even with a green banner, extremely long continuous play is a common reason for bans.

> [!WARNING]
> If you repeatedly run 12 plus hour cycles, you can be banned on session length patterns alone.

## How to bot with PS

We cannot provide step by step instructions for unattended botting, or guidance focused on bypassing detection.

What we can tell you:

- Use PS as an assist tool while you are actively playing, unless you accept the risk of botting
- If you bot, use **PSB** , and confirm it with the Bot Bypass Checker before every session
- Avoid long continuous sessions and repetitive schedules
- Prefer short, variable sessions with real breaks
