# Developer Program

> Source: <https://docs.project-sylvanas.net/dev/developer-program>

## 1. Getting Started

Anyone can begin developing for our platform right away. All the necessary documentation and tools are publicly available, so you can freely experiment, create, and test your scripts without any special roles or permissions.

If you later decide you’d like to publish your scripts or earn money from them, you’ll need to apply for the **Trial Developer** role.

## 2. Trial Developer

Developers who already have a **portfolio or reputation** (for example, in WoW or a similar ecosystem) can be granted **Trial Developer** status upfront for a **grace period of up to 30 days**.

To become a Trial Developer, simply show us your work, either new scripts you’ve made for our platform or examples from your past experience. Once reviewed and approved, you’ll receive the role and can start publishing your projects immediately.

The **Trial Developer** role is the first official step toward joining our development team. It grants permission to **publish** your scripts and **use PS** for free during a limited **grace period**.

## 3. Developer

After being an active Trial Developer for a while, you can advance to **Developer** status. This is a more **permanent position** that provides **unlimited PS time** and reflects a deeper level of trust and commitment.

We aim to make sure this role is earned through **consistent effort and meaningful contributions**, not just by being around briefly. This stage also acts as the gateway to the next level of recognition and benefits.

## 4. Senior / Veteran Developer

The **Senior** or **Veteran Developer** role is reserved for long-term, highly active developers who have consistently delivered quality and innovation. This role comes with extra **perks**, including free unlimited access for **Private PS**.

## 5. Revenue Split and Platform Services

Our revenue split is **70/30** (Developer/Platform).

The 30% funds everything that isn’t writing your script, and keeps your work secure, visible, and profitable.

**Protection.** Every plugin is encrypted and obfuscated before it ships, on top of our authentication system. No PS plugin has ever been compromised in more than two years of operation.

**Distribution and updates.** Hosting, the update pipeline, and version delivery to every user. You push a build and it reaches your users.

**Changelog broadcasts.** You write your release notes and our bot posts them to Discord automatically, in front of an audience of over 5,000 users. Every update works like a newsletter to people already interested in your work.

**Marketing and visibility.** Storefront placement, launch and update announcements, and an established user base that is already here. A new script isn’t launching into an empty room.

**Payments and risk.** Transaction management, chargeback protection, and guaranteed payouts.

**Developer Panel.** A dashboard for managing your scripts, updates, and earnings in one place.

We handle the infrastructure, risk, and backend complexity. Your only focus is building and refining your creations.

## 6. Becoming a Developer

If you’d like to become a developer or have questions about the process, please **open a ticket in Discord** and speak with our **staff members**.

Due to the rapid growth of **AI-assisted development**, we’ve received hundreds of code review requests and can no longer offer individual code reviews as part of the application process.

We are also much more selective when reviewing applications from developers we do not already know. The requirements for **Trial Developer** access must be discussed directly with staff through your Discord ticket.
